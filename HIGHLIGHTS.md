# Highlights

- Evaluated fixed five-qubit reservoir residuals on 22 official TDC ADMET endpoints.
- Matched quantum readouts against equal-budget real-orthogonal quadratic controls.
- Known-signal calibration recovered QRC and classical targets in at least 99.3% of simulations, with null false-positive rates of 2.0-4.7%.
- Found no positive endpoint after Holm correction on two distinct classical bases.
- Joint gating, low-data, finite-shot, simulated-noise, and reservoir-family search did not reverse the result.
- External hERG and BBB results showed source-dependent transfer rather than a general advantage.
- Continuous-label rank transfer produced only an unadjusted CatBoost/hERG candidate signal, with no effect after correction across all exploratory comparisons.
- External effect decomposition, calibration, chemical-space visualization, and an independent pIC50 regression bound the local signal.
- A scaffold-disjoint, directly trained ToxTree pIC50 cohort also showed no corrected QRC advantage on either base.
- Explicit Qiskit/Aer circuits showed strong readout attenuation under gate and readout errors while retaining high feature-rank correlation.
